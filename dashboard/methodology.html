BIS IS 10500 Scoring Model

The core algorithmic engine derives its severity model from the Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS IS 10500). Parameters are heavily weighted based on acute physiological hazard.

  • Arsenic 1.0
  • E. coli 0.9
  • Fluoride 0.8
  • Nitrate 0.7
  • Iron 0.4
  • Turbidity / TDS 0.3
  • pH 0.2

Data Acquisition & Pipeline

The backend relies on a highly-optimized, multi-threaded WQMIS crawler extracting live water quality laboratory reports across Uttar Pradesh and Bihar.
