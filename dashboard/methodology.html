BIS IS 10500 Scoring Model

WaterTriage uses the Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) IS 10500:2012 drinking water specification as the regulatory baseline. Each sample is scored across eight scored parameters using weighted severity interpolation between acceptable and permissible limits.

Data Sources

The primary dataset consists of 1,741 scored water samples from Uttar Pradesh and Bihar, sourced from the JJM Water Quality Management Information System (WQMIS). Historical context is provided by 100,690 contamination events from the WQMIS national Format L1 datasets (2009–2023).
